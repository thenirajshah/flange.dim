IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Reference
notes.

Keep these source notes in mind when reviewing the displayed values.

01

Raised face

The 1.6 mm raised face is included in the thickness and length of the flange, as stated in the source reference.

02

Welding neck dimension

The marked dimension denotes the outside diameter (O.D.) of the pipe in a welding neck flange.

03

ANSI B16.5 coverage

The 22″ size is marked in the source as not covered by ANSI B16.5. Check the applicable standard before using it for design or procurement.
